01

Direction — correspondence desk

The subject

Elsewhere is a private place to sustain character-led conversations and creative writing. Its primary job is not to expose controls; it is to make a long exchange easy to enter, read and continue.

Design decision: make the current conversation the centre of gravity. The interface becomes a desk: a slim signal rail for global movement, a quiet character library for context, and one broad writing stage. The cyan relay edge is a functional place marker, not decoration.

05

Self-critique and guardrails

Generic neon risk
MED
Violet and cyan can become a stock cyberpunk skin. Keep the glow limited to the wordmark and active relay edge; no grids or halo effects behind messages.
Card-kit risk
MED
Do not divide every setting or message into equal rounded cards. The structure comes from the desk layers, spacing and one active edge.
